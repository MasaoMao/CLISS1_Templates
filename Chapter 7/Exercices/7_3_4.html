Exercise 7.3.4 – FizzBuzz

This page contains two implementations of Fizz/Buzz:

  • Version A: print "Fizz" for numbers divisible by 3, "Buzz" for numbers divisible by 5 (but not 3), otherwise the number.
  • Version B: print "FizzBuzz" for numbers divisible by both 3 and 5; otherwise "Fizz" or "Buzz" as appropriate.
